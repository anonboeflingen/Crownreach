Crownreach is divided into four major realms and the largely unruled Western Wilds. Each region has its own culture, naming style, government, and atmosphere.

Takamori is the eastern realm. Its culture and naming are Japanese-toned, with feudal noble houses, samurai-like warriors, sword schools, rice-growing valleys, shrines, fortified settlements, and mountain roads. Its sovereign is regarded as a sacred symbol of the realm, and the imperial court is highly ceremonial, restricted, and hierarchical.

Vardenwyck is the central medieval kingdom. Its culture blends English, Germanic, and Slavic influences, with castles, villages, forests, farmland, knightly houses, monasteries, old roads, and feudal politics. Its tone ranges from Arthurian courtly fantasy to harsher Witcher-like rural life. Latin or Latin-derived language may appear in religion, law, scholarship, heraldry, and knightly institutions.

Qadareth is the southern desert realm. Its language and names have an Arabic and Chakobsa-like sound. Civilization is sparse, with immense distances of desert between rare settlements. Cities, temples, and fortresses may be monumental, subterranean, or built into mountains around scarce water sources. Qadareth is defined as much by emptiness and distance as by its ancient architecture.

Dunskeld is the northern land of independent jarls. Its culture blends Old Norse/Icelandic traditions with Celtic influence. The jarls feud and rule separately, but when a major external war threatens Dunskeld they may proclaim a single wartime king whose authority lasts only for the duration of the war.

The Western Wilds have no unified kingdom. They contain remote settlements, ruins, old roads, forests, highlands, forgotten strongholds, and frontier communities beyond the stable control of the major realms.
